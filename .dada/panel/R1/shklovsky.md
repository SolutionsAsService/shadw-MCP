# R1 - Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky)

- **Anchor used:** "Art as Device": art exists to make the stone *stony*, to defeat automatized perception by roughening the form (ostranenie), so the thing is seen, not just recognized.

- **Observed:**
  - index-375.png: the hero is a vertical stack of five dark rounded cards with a big number left, a small caps label and a grey caption. The stat-tile row every SaaS landing has; the eye reads "features" and slides past. Three of five numbers are the same automatized gesture: "1 key to set", "1 call to roll back", "3 verdicts".
  - The pink-to-blue ramp (#e92a67 to #2a8af6, index.html line 27) on pure black, with gradient-filled verbs in the h1, is the house dialect of developer-tool landings.
  - The one estranged element is "600 seconds it waits for the build". A wait shown as a monument makes the agent's time felt. It still sits in a card identical to the "1" cards, so it gets flattened.
  - The 15-cell matrix (line 71) is the strangest and truest device. The single hatched yellow cell (`revoke_api_key`, destructive) makes danger stony. But it is ~16 px squares at the very bottom of the first screen, below five loud cards: the device is buried.
  - index-1280.png is a broken capture (hero tiled 2x2, nav missing).

- **Verdict:** OBJECT

- **Requested change:** Invert the hierarchy. Make the 15-cell matrix the dominant hero object, with each cell large enough to carry its tool name, and make the one destructive cell the single loud, physically different thing on the page. Demote the five stage tiles to a thin wire with labels. Keep exactly one big number, 600, drawn as duration: a wire segment whose length is proportional to the wait, not a card. Drop the gradient fill from the h1 verbs so the ramp lives only on the wire. Recapture index-1280.png.
