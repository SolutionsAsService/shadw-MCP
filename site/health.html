3 · Health

3 verdicts, each with its reasons.

shadw_project_health reads three things and returns one verdict: down, degraded, healthy, with the reasons. It is a check you run, not a monitor: nothing runs between calls. server/src/tools.js:62

  1. down when the project has no production deployment, or its production URL does not answer below HTTP 500. server/src/tools.js:92
  2. degraded when the last build ended in error or cancelled, or 5xx responses were logged in the window (default 1h; also 30m, 24h, 7d). server/src/tools.js:87
  3. healthy otherwise.

Two other outcomes: a project the key's team does not have is an error, not a verdict server/src/tools.js:66; and when the logs cannot be read, the reason says so and the verdict is at least degraded server/src/tools.js:89.
